Manhattan's Woman-Owned Interior Design Studio, Led by Samantha Abrams

Mr. Interiors Design Studio is a New York–based interior design atelier founded and led by Samantha Abrams, a licensed real estate professional with a technical foundation in how spaces are developed and used. For Manhattan clients searching for a woman-owned luxury interior design firm, the studio offers a distinct combination: a female founder's eye for structure and proportion, paired with a design language built specifically around clarity, restraint, and complete execution.

Mr. Interiors serves clients across New York, New Jersey, the Hamptons, Brooklyn, Manhattan, and Florida (including Miami), with specializations in commercial, residential, apartment, townhouse, and single-family home projects.

Founded by a Woman with a Technical Foundation in Real Estate

Samantha Abrams studied real estate at the NYU-Schack Institute of Real Estate, giving her a technical foundation in how spaces are developed and used — a perspective that goes beyond aesthetics into how a property actually functions. She is also a licensed real estate professional, and she brings operational experience from residential and commercial environments where precision, coordination, and integrity were essential.

Raised by an interior designer, Samantha developed an early understanding of how layout, proportion, material, and light shape daily life. That background informs a design philosophy guided by structure and clarity rather than trend, with every project approached as a complete system rather than a collection of individual pieces.

A Studio Built Around Decisive, Architectural Design

The studio's design language is clean, restrained, and architectural, often informed by mid-century modern principles. Rather than presenting clients with endless options, Mr. Interiors emphasizes intelligent planning, curated selections, and decisive editing — reducing friction for the client while improving the final result.

This approach is delivered as a concierge model: thoughtful planning, refined selection, and complete execution from layout and concept through sourcing, logistics, and installation.
