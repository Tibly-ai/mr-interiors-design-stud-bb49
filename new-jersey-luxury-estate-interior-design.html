Led by a Female Founder: Samantha Abrams' Approach to New Jersey Custom Estates

Mr. Interiors Design Studio was founded by Samantha Abrams. Raised by an interior designer, Samantha developed an early understanding of how space functions and how layout, proportion, material, and light shape daily life. Her work is guided by structure and clarity rather than trend.

Samantha studied real estate at NYU-Schack Institute of Real Estate, giving her a technical foundation in how spaces are developed and used, and she brings operational experience from residential and commercial environments where precision, coordination, and integrity were essential. She is also a licensed real estate professional — a distinction that informs how the studio evaluates a New Jersey estate's layout, value, and long-term function, not just its finishes.
