Boutique Commercial & Office Interior Design in New York

Mr. Interiors Design Studio is a New York–based interior design atelier serving clients across New York, New Jersey, the Hamptons, Brooklyn, Manhattan, and Florida, including Miami. For businesses seeking a boutique interior design firm for upscale offices and commercial spaces, the studio applies its concierge model — thoughtful planning, refined selection, and complete execution — to commercial environments, built around a clean, restrained, architectural design language often informed by mid-century modern principles.

This page outlines how that approach applies specifically to boutique retail and executive office projects in New York, and answers the questions commercial clients ask most before starting a project.

Full-Service Commercial Interior Design, Managed End to End

Mr. Interiors specializes in Commercial, Residential, Apartments, Townhouse, and Single Family Home projects. On the commercial side, this means the studio is equipped to take on office and boutique retail environments of varying scope — from a single considered space to a full build-out — with one team managing the process from concept to installation.

The studio's concierge approach is designed for business owners and operators who want a refined, complete commercial space without managing the process themselves. Decisions are guided, options are curated rather than endless, and execution — sourcing, logistics, and installation — is handled in full. This structure is intended to reduce friction for principals and decision-makers who need a finished, functioning space without absorbing the day-to-day coordination themselves.

A Real Estate–Trained Approach to Commercial Space

Founder Samantha Abrams studied real estate at NYU-Schack Institute of Real Estate, providing a technical foundation in how spaces are developed and used. She brings operational experience from residential and commercial environments, where precision, coordination, and integrity were essential. She is also a licensed real estate professional.

This background shapes how the studio approaches commercial work: each project is treated as a complete system — layout, proportion, material, and light considered together — rather than a collection of individual pieces. For a boutique office or retail environment, that means the space is planned around how it will actually function and develop over time, not styled after the fact.

Design Language for Executive Offices & Boutique Retail

The studio's design language is clean, restrained, and architectural, often informed by mid-century modern principles. Emphasis is placed on intelligent planning, curated selections, and decisive editing so clients are presented with a refined set of options rather than endless choices.

For upscale offices and boutique commercial spaces, this translates to:

  • Intelligent planning — layout decisions made with an understanding of how the space is used and how business real estate performs, informed by Samantha's NYU-Schack training.
  • Curated selection — a refined set of material, furnishing, and finish options rather than open-ended catalogs, so decisions move quickly.
  • Complete execution — sourcing, logistics, and installation handled by one team, from concept through final walkthrough.
