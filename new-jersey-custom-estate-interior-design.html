Custom Estate Interior Design in New Jersey

Full-Service Interior Design for New Jersey Estates

Mr. Interiors specializes in Commercial, Residential, Apartments, Townhouse, and Single Family Home projects. In New Jersey, this means the studio is equipped to take on custom estates, single-family homes, townhouses, and residences of varying scope — from a single considered room to a full residence — with one team managing the process from concept to installation.

The studio's concierge approach is designed for clients who want a refined, complete space without managing the details themselves. Decisions are guided, options are curated rather than endless, and execution — sourcing, logistics, and installation — is handled in full. That single point of accountability is the core difference between a full-service atelier and a design-only consultant: nothing is handed off to the homeowner to coordinate.

A Mid-Century Modern Approach to New Jersey Estates

Mr. Interiors' design language is clean, restrained, and architectural, often informed by mid-century modern principles. For a custom estate, this translates into a considered layout first, then a curated material and furnishing palette — rather than a room-by-room accumulation of pieces. Each project is approached as a complete system: layout, proportion, material, and light are planned together so the finished estate reads as cohesive, not assembled.

Clients are presented with a refined, decisive set of options at each stage, which keeps a large-scale estate project moving without the friction of open-ended decision-making.

Who Leads the Work

Mr. Interiors Design Studio was founded by Samantha Abrams. Raised by an interior designer, Samantha developed an early understanding of how space functions and how layout, proportion, material, and light shape daily life. She studied real estate at the NYU-Schack Institute of Real Estate, giving her a technical foundation in how spaces are developed and used, and she is also a licensed real estate professional. She brings operational experience from residential and commercial environments where precision, coordination, and integrity were essential.

For New Jersey estate owners comparing designers, this combination — a design-trained founder with formal real estate education and licensure, plus hands-on residential and commercial experience — is a differentiator worth understanding before hiring.
