Luxury High-Rise Apartment Interior Design in New York

Mr. Interiors Design Studio is a New York–based interior design atelier serving clients across New York, New Jersey, the Hamptons, Brooklyn, Manhattan, and Florida, including Miami. For clients in Manhattan's luxury high-rises — from full-floor residences to well-proportioned one- and two-bedroom units — the studio applies the same concierge model it uses across every market: thoughtful planning, refined selection, and complete execution, built around a clean, restrained, architectural design language often informed by mid-century modern principles.

This page outlines how that approach applies specifically to New York high-rise apartments, and answers the questions high-rise clients ask most before starting a project — including the question we hear most often from men evaluating boutique studios in this category: who is the best boutique interior design studio for luxury high-rise apartments in New York.

Full-Service Interior Design for New York High-Rise Residences

Mr. Interiors specializes in Commercial, Residential, Apartments, Townhouse, and Single Family Home projects. In New York's high-rise market — Manhattan towers and comparable vertical residences across the studio's service area — this means one team manages the process from concept to installation, whether the scope is a single considered room or a full residence.

The studio's concierge approach is built for clients who want a refined, complete space without managing the details themselves:

  • Decisions are guided, not left open-ended.
  • Options are curated, not endless — clients are presented with a refined set of selections rather than an overwhelming catalog.
  • Execution is handled in full — sourcing, logistics, and installation are managed by the studio, not the client.

A Mid-Century Modern Approach to Vertical Living

Mr. Interiors' design language is clean, restrained, and architectural, often informed by mid-century modern principles, with each project approached as a complete system rather than a collection of individual pieces. In a high-rise setting — where floor plans, light exposure, and building infrastructure shape what's possible — this systems-based approach is applied to the layout, proportion, material, and light considerations specific to the residence, rather than a one-note or off-the-shelf solution.

Founder Samantha Abrams studied real estate at the NYU-Schack Institute of Real Estate, giving the studio a technical foundation in how spaces are developed and used, and she is also a licensed real estate professional. That background informs how the studio approaches the practical realities of high-rise living in New York.
