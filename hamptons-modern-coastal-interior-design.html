A Mid-Century Modern Approach to Coastal Renovation

Mr. Interiors' design language is clean, restrained, and architectural, often informed by mid-century modern principles. Each project is approached as a complete system — layout, proportion, material, and light working together — rather than a collection of individual pieces. For a Hamptons coastal property, this means structural planning and intelligent layout decisions come first, with curated material and furnishing selections built around that foundation, rather than a room-by-room, showroom-style approach.
