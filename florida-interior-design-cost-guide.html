How Much Does a Full-Service Luxury Interior Designer Cost in Florida?

Mr. Interiors Design Studio is a New York–based interior design atelier serving clients across New York, New Jersey, the Hamptons, Brooklyn, Manhattan, and Florida, including Miami. For Florida clients — from Miami condos to single-family homes — investment typically falls into one of three ranges, based on scope, square footage, and how much of the process (planning, sourcing, procurement, installation) is fully managed by the studio.

Quick answer: Full-service interior design with Mr. Interiors in Florida generally starts at $5,000 and can extend to $50,000+, depending on scope. Clients without a set budget in mind can work with the studio to define scope and investment together during the initial consultation.

What Affects Your Price in Florida

The following factors move a Florida project from one budget tier to the next:

  • Scope of the space — a single room versus a full condo, townhouse, or single-family home.
  • Square footage — larger Florida properties, including Miami condos and single-family homes, require more planning and sourcing.
  • Level of service managed by the studio — planning only versus full-service sourcing, procurement, and installation.
  • Property type — Mr. Interiors specializes in Commercial, Residential, Apartments, Townhouse, and Single Family Home projects, and Florida engagements can fall into any of these categories.
  • Timeline and start date — project intake includes a preferred start date, which can affect sourcing lead times and overall investment.

What "Full-Service" Includes

Mr. Interiors applies the same concierge model in Florida that it uses across every market it serves: thoughtful planning, refined selection, and complete execution — built around a clean, restrained, architectural design language often informed by mid-century modern principles. For Florida clients, this means:

  • One team managing the process from concept to installation, whether the project is a single considered room or a full residence.
  • A curated, decisive set of options rather than endless choices, so decisions are efficient and informed.
  • Full execution — sourcing, logistics, and installation — handled by the studio rather than left to the client to coordinate.
