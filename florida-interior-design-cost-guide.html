What's Included in a Full-Service Project

Every Florida engagement — regardless of budget tier — draws from the same full-service scope offered across all markets:

  • Space planning and layout
  • Concept development and design direction
  • Furniture, lighting, and material selection
  • Custom sourcing and procurement
  • Vendor coordination and logistics
  • Styling, installation, and final walkthrough

This is what separates a full-service engagement from a showroom or à la carte design service: the studio manages the entire process end to end, from initial layout to the final styled walkthrough, so clients are not coordinating vendors, tracking shipments, or making dozens of individual purchasing decisions themselves.

What Affects Your Price in Florida

Several factors determine where a Florida project lands within these ranges:

1. Scope of service selected. A project limited to space planning and concept direction costs less than one that also includes full sourcing, procurement, vendor coordination, and installation management.

2. Number of rooms or total square footage. Single-room projects (a primary bedroom, a home office) sit at the lower end. Full apartments, townhouses, and single-family homes move toward the higher end.

3. Level of custom sourcing required. Projects built around curated, non-showroom selections require more sourcing and procurement work than projects using in-stock pieces.

4. Property type. Apartments, townhouses, and single-family homes each carry different logistics — elevator restrictions, delivery windows, and installation complexity in Florida buildings can affect vendor coordination time.

5. Commercial vs. residential. Mr. Interiors serves both commercial and residential clients; commercial scopes are evaluated individually based on square footage and use.
