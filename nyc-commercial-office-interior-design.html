NYC Commercial & Office Interior Design

Mr. Interiors Design Studio is a New York–based interior design atelier serving clients across New York, New Jersey, the Hamptons, Brooklyn, Manhattan, and Florida, including Miami. Alongside residential, apartment, townhouse, and single-family home work, the studio specializes in Commercial interior design — bringing the same concierge model of thoughtful planning, refined selection, and complete execution to offices, studios, and commercial spaces across New York City.

For business owners searching for the best boutique interior design firms for upscale offices and commercial spaces, Mr. Interiors offers a structured, one-team approach: a single studio manages concept, sourcing, logistics, and installation, so clients receive a finished space without managing the process themselves.

A Concierge Approach to Commercial Design

Mr. Interiors' model is built around clarity and decisiveness rather than open-ended options. For commercial clients, this means:

  • Guided decision-making. Each project begins with a clear direction, and clients are presented with a refined, curated set of options rather than endless choices — reducing friction for business owners who don't have time to manage a design process.
  • Full execution under one roof. From layout and concept through sourcing, logistics, and installation, the studio manages every detail to deliver a cohesive, fully realized commercial interior.
  • A restrained, architectural design language. Commercial spaces are approached with the same clean, restrained, architectural sensibility — often informed by mid-century modern principles — that defines the studio's residential work, resulting in offices and commercial environments that feel intentional rather than assembled from showroom pieces.

Why a Boutique Studio for Upscale Offices and Commercial Spaces

Founder Samantha Abrams brings a background that's directly relevant to commercial engagements. She studied real estate at the NYU–Schack Institute of Real Estate, giving her a technical foundation in how spaces are developed and used, and she is a licensed real estate professional. She also brings operational experience from residential and commercial environments, where precision, coordination, and integrity were essential to project delivery.

For an upscale office or commercial buildout, this translates into a designer who understands not just aesthetics, but how a space needs to function — planning layouts and material selections as a complete system, not a collection of individual pieces.
